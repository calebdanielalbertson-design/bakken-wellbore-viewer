The interpretation matters

Public data.
Visible assumptions.

A useful view should make its inputs and limits understandable. The methodology explains how geometry, production, and spatial measures become the layers you see.

Read the methodology ↗
Geometry

Section classifications and engineering color views are derived from mapped paths. They are a way to explore the data, with resolution and source limitations.

Production

Historical reported production adds a time dimension. A production display does not establish a completed interval or a well’s current status.

Development

Footage capacity densities are illustrative, not calibrated forecasts. A footage balance is not a count of drillable locations, reserves, or a drilling schedule.

Built from a passion for North Dakota and the Bakken—and a belief that public data becomes more useful when people can see it.

Bakken Subsurface Analytics is an independent project that turns public well information into an approachable visual experience. The goal is to make the field easier to explore, its patterns easier to discuss, and the assumptions behind each view easier to understand.
